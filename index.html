A cube with
room to move.

A polished, independent interpretation of the iconic Resend cube. Drag to rotate it, or leave it to find its own rhythm.

Drag anywhere on the cube to change its view.

This project grew out of Peter’s Devs Love Coffee tutorial, and early iterations used and refined its example code. The public version has since been substantially rewritten and extended by Dario Simic / RevoDev.

Independent project. Not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Resend.
